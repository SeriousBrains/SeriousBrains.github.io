What's inside the game

Three connected modes built around one loop: keep playing, keep learning, without one feeling like the price of the other.

🏔️

A campaign for grammar

Climb levels one after another, the way you'd progress through any mobile game. Each level builds on the last, so the grammar lands while you're focused on the next step.

🌍

Two vocabulary biomes

Dedicated worlds where each word is learned together with the context it lives in. You walk through environments where the words make sense and stay.

⚔️

Multiplayer matches

Race opponents at your own level in real-time word and sentence matches. The French you've built becomes the thing you race with.

Honest about the demo

This is a real game and the loop works. It is not the final version.

The multiplayer is simulated for now. Matches feel real, but you're racing scripted opponents while we gather the feedback to know what to build the actual multiplayer around. The real multiplayer arrives once the format proves itself.

The demo is Android only, French only, and free. It covers a few starting points designed for complete beginners, enough to let you judge whether the concept is worth building further. The full learning path arrives in the final version.

If you play it, the most useful thing you can do is tell us what works and what doesn't.
